BOSCH WASHING MACHINE ERROR CODES - TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Model: WAJ2846IIN, WAK2406IIN, WAT2846IIN
Series: Bosch Serie 4 & Serie 6 Front Load Washing Machines

===========================================
COMMON ERROR CODES AND SOLUTIONS
===========================================

ERROR CODE: E18
Description: Drainage Problem - Water is not draining from the machine
Possible Causes:
1. Blocked drain hose
2. Clogged drain filter
3. Faulty drain pump
4. Kinked drainage pipe

TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS:
Step 1: Check the drain hose
- Ensure the drain hose is not kinked or bent
- Verify the hose is positioned correctly (not more than 1 meter high)
- Check for blockages inside the hose

Step 2: Clean the drain filter
- Open the service flap at the bottom front of the machine
- Place a shallow container underneath
- Turn the drain filter counter-clockwise to remove
- Clean any debris, lint, or foreign objects
- Re-install the filter and close the flap

Step 3: Inspect the drain pump
- If steps 1-2 don't resolve the issue, the drain pump may be faulty
- Listen for unusual noises from the pump area
- Contact authorized Bosch service center

Step 4: Check drainage pipe connection
- Ensure the sink/drain pipe is not clogged
- Remove any blockages from the connection point

===========================================

ERROR CODE: E17
Description: Water Inlet Problem - Water is not filling properly
Possible Causes:
1. Water tap is closed or water pressure is too low
2. Inlet hose is kinked or blocked
3. Inlet filter is clogged
4. Faulty inlet valve

TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS:
Step 1: Check water supply
- Ensure the water tap is fully open
- Verify adequate water pressure (minimum 1 bar, maximum 10 bar)

Step 2: Inspect inlet hose
- Check for kinks or bends in the hose
- Ensure hose connections are tight

Step 3: Clean inlet filter
- Turn off water supply
- Disconnect inlet hose from the machine
- Remove and clean the filter mesh at the inlet valve
- Reconnect hose and turn on water supply

Step 4: Test inlet valve
- If problem persists, inlet valve may be faulty
- Contact authorized service center

===========================================

ERROR CODE: E02
Description: Motor Problem
Possible Causes:
1. Overloaded drum
2. Unbalanced load
3. Motor malfunction

TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS:
Step 1: Check load size
- Remove some items if drum is overloaded
- Maximum load: 8kg for Serie 6, 6kg for Serie 4

Step 2: Redistribute clothes
- Open door and rearrange clothes evenly
- Ensure heavy items are mixed with lighter ones

Step 3: Reset machine
- Turn off machine
- Wait 30 seconds
- Turn back on and restart cycle

Step 4: Contact service
- If error persists, motor may need inspection
- Call Bosch service center

===========================================

ERROR CODE: E04
Description: Unbalanced Load Detection
Solution:
- Open the door and redistribute the laundry evenly
- Ensure the machine is level (use spirit level)
- Adjust machine feet if necessary
- Don't wash single heavy items alone (add towels to balance)

===========================================

ERROR CODE: E23
Description: Water in Base Tray (Leak Detection)
Possible Causes:
1. Leak in inlet/drain hose
2. Damaged door seal
3. Loose hose connections

TROUBLESHOOTING STEPS:
Step 1: Turn off water supply immediately
Step 2: Unplug the machine
Step 3: Check all hose connections for leaks
Step 4: Inspect door seal for damage
Step 5: Contact service center - DO NOT use machine until repaired

===========================================

GENERAL MAINTENANCE TIPS:

1. CLEAN DRAIN FILTER MONTHLY
   - Prevents E18 errors
   - Improves drainage performance
   - Extends pump life

2. CHECK INLET FILTER QUARTERLY
   - Prevents E17 errors
   - Maintains water flow
   - Protects inlet valve

3. CLEAN DETERGENT DRAWER REGULARLY
   - Remove and rinse under running water
   - Prevents detergent buildup
   - Improves wash quality

4. WIPE DOOR SEAL AFTER EACH WASH
   - Prevents mold and odor
   - Extends seal life
   - Maintains hygiene

5. RUN TUB CLEAN CYCLE MONTHLY
   - Use Bosch Washing Machine Cleaner
   - Run at 90°C empty cycle
   - Removes detergent residue and bacteria
