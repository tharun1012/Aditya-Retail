## ❌ WHAT DOESN'T WORK (AND IS ILLEGAL)

### **Direct Scraping from Social Media**

**You CANNOT do this:**
```
❌ Scraping Facebook profiles
❌ Scraping Instagram user data
❌ Scraping LinkedIn contacts
❌ Scraping Google My Business listings
❌ Scraping WhatsApp numbers
```

**Why NOT?**

1. **Illegal** - Violates Computer Fraud and Abuse Act
2. **Terms of Service Violation** - Will get you banned
3. **GDPR/Privacy Laws** - Can lead to heavy fines
4. **Technical Barriers** - Platforms actively block scrapers
5. **Poor Quality** - Scraped leads are cold, uninterested

**Real Consequences:**
- Facebook/Instagram: Permanent account ban
- LinkedIn: Account suspension + legal action
- Google: Business profile removal
- Personal liability: Fines up to ₹50 Lakhs in India

## 🔐 COMPLIANCE & PRIVACY

### **What You MUST Do:**

**1. Privacy Policy**
- Clearly state how you collect data
- How you use it
- How users can opt out

**2. Consent**
- Get explicit consent before contacting
- "I agree to receive marketing emails"
- GDPR/India privacy law compliant

**3. Opt-Out Mechanism**
- Every email has unsubscribe link
- Honor opt-out requests immediately
- Delete data if requested (GDPR Right to be Forgotten)

**4. Data Security**
- Encrypt customer data
- Secure database access
- Regular backups
- No data sharing without consent
